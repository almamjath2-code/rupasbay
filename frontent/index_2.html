Our Story

Fed by the same wave that brought the surfers in.

Rupa's Bay sits where the sand meets the tables — a short walk from Baby Point and Main Point, the surf breaks that put Arugam Bay on the map. What started as a simple kitchen serving big plates to hungry surfers has grown into a full restaurant and bar, without losing the reason people first came: honest food, cold drinks, and a view of the water.

Our menu leans into what's local — rice, curry, fresh reef fish — alongside international dishes and vegan options for every kind of traveller who ends up at our tables.
