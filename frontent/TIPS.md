# Admin Dashboard - Tips & Tricks

## 🎯 Pro Tips for Managing Your Restaurant

### Dashboard
- **Tip 1**: Check the Dashboard regularly to see at a glance:
  - How many menu items you have
  - Pending reservations that need approval
  - New reviews coming in
  - Upcoming events
  
- **Tip 2**: The Recent Activity section shows the latest changes, helping you stay updated without checking each section

- **Tip 3**: Hover over the stat cards to see if they highlight - this indicates they're clickable in future versions

### Menu Management

#### Adding Menu Items
- **Pro Tip**: Always add high-quality images for menu items - they're displayed in your public website
- **Chef Pick**: Use this to highlight your signature dishes or specials
- **Categories**: Keep category names consistent (e.g., "Appetizers", "Main Courses", "Desserts") for better filtering
- **Availability**: Quickly toggle availability if an item runs out

#### Searching & Filtering
- **Quick Search**: Type to instantly search across all menu items
- **Filter by Category**: Use the dropdown to view specific categories
- **Combine Filters**: Search within a category for precise results
- **Sort Tip**: Items are sorted alphabetically - use naming conventions for better organization

#### Editing Menu Items
- **Edit Quickly**: Click the edit button to modify prices or availability
- **Update Image**: Re-upload an image to update a menu item's photo
- **Price Changes**: Change prices directly without re-creating items
- **Keep Descriptions Fresh**: Update descriptions regularly with seasonal notes

### Reservation Management

#### Organizing Reservations
- **Status Tracking**: Use the status filter to see:
  - **Pending**: New bookings awaiting your approval
  - **Approved**: Confirmed reservations
  - **Cancelled**: Cancelled bookings (archived)

- **Peak Times**: Use the date/time info to identify busy periods
- **Special Requests**: Always check the Special Requests field - customers often have dietary needs

#### Workflow
1. Check pending reservations first
2. Approve reservations you can accommodate
3. Contact customers for approval confirmation
4. Change status to "Approved"
5. Delete cancelled bookings regularly

### Review Management

#### Building Trust
- **Approve Reviews**: Show positive reviews to build social proof
- **Respond Professionally**: Delete any inappropriate reviews
- **Monitor Ratings**: Track your average rating over time
- **Photo Reviews**: Reviews with photos are more impactful - encourage photo uploads

#### Best Practices
- Approve reviews within 24 hours
- Respond to feedback professionally
- Share positive reviews on social media
- Use feedback to improve menu and service

### Events Management

#### Creating Exciting Events
- **Upcoming Events**: Create events to promote special dining experiences
- **Image Quality**: Use high-quality event photos
- **Lead Time**: Create events 2-3 weeks in advance
- **Promotion**: Events show on your public website - make them attractive

#### Event Ideas
- Wine pairing nights
- Seasonal menu launches
- Live music performances
- Chef's table experiences
- Holiday celebrations
- Corporate dining events

### Gallery Management

#### Visual Storytelling
- **Showcase Quality**: Upload only your best restaurant photos
- **Variety**: Include:
  - Dining room ambiance
  - Plated dishes
  - Chef at work
  - Happy customers
  - Outdoor seating
  
- **Lighting**: Good lighting makes a huge difference
- **Resolution**: Use high-resolution images (at least 1200x800px)

#### Organization
- **Upload Regularly**: Keep your gallery fresh with recent photos
- **Seasonal Changes**: Update photos to reflect seasonal décor
- **Social Proof**: These photos are used to attract new customers

### Settings

#### Restaurant Information
- **Name & Address**: Keep this current - used for maps and directions
- **Hours**: Update during holidays and special hours
- **Contact**: Make sure phone and email are monitored
- **Social Media**: Link to your active social accounts

#### Social Media Strategy
- **Instagram**: Link to attract food photographers and reviews
- **Facebook**: Keep event details linked
- **TripAdvisor**: Direct reviews there for SEO benefit
- **Google Maps**: Helps with local search visibility

## 🔐 Security Reminders

### API Key Safety
- **Never Share**: Your API key is your password - never share it
- **Logout**: Always logout when done
- **Public Computers**: Don't access the dashboard on public WiFi
- **Change Regularly**: Consider regenerating your API key periodically

### Data Protection
- **HTTPS**: Use HTTPS if accessing over the internet
- **Unique Password**: If available, use a unique password for your account
- **Two-Factor**: Enable 2FA if your backend supports it

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Forgetting to Approve Reservations**
   - Set a daily reminder to check pending reservations

2. **Outdated Menu Items**
   - Delete unavailable items instead of leaving them available
   - Update prices when they change

3. **Ignoring Reviews**
   - Respond to reviews professionally
   - Don't leave reviews pending for weeks

4. **Low-Quality Photos**
   - Use good lighting
   - Use high-resolution images
   - Show your best work

5. **Inconsistent Information**
   - Keep address and hours updated
   - Use consistent category names
   - Maintain professional tone

6. **Forgetting Logout**
   - Always logout when done
   - Especially on shared computers
